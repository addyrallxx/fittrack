Free offline fitness tracking, on your phone

FitTrack is a fitness tracker PWA with no subscription and no account. Log workouts, food, weight, water and reminders. Your history stays in your browser, and the app keeps working offline after your first visit.

Open FitTrack free No signup. No app store.

What makes FitTrack different

Private by default

Your food, workout and weight history stays in your browser. There is no profile to create.

Useful offline

Install it once, then keep logging in a basement gym, on a plane or anywhere with a weak connection.

Flexible training days

Complete three interchangeable full-body sessions on the days that work for you.

GLP-1 calorie-floor support

People tracking retatrutide, semaglutide, tirzepatide or another GLP-1 can use an optional calorie-floor warning and weekly dose reminder. FitTrack never recommends a dose.

How to add FitTrack to your Android home screen

Use Chrome for the smoothest install on Android.

  1. Open FitTrack using the button above in Chrome.

  2. Tap Chrome's three-dot menu.

  3. Tap "Install app."

    Some phones show "Add to Home screen" instead. Either option creates a FitTrack icon.

  4. Open FitTrack from the new icon.

    Go to Settings inside FitTrack if you want to enable notification reminders.

Notifications also work in a regular Chrome tab. Installing gives FitTrack its own icon and a full-screen app window.

How to add FitTrack to an iPhone or iPad

Use Safari and complete the steps in order. Home Screen installation is required for notifications on iPhone and iPad.

Open the installed app before enabling notifications. A regular Safari tab cannot receive web push notifications on iPhone or iPad.

  1. Open the FitTrack link above in Safari.

    If it opened inside Instagram, WhatsApp or Messages, use that app's menu to open the page in Safari.

  2. Tap the Share icon Share in Safari's toolbar.

  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen," then tap "Add."

    A FitTrack icon appears on your Home Screen.

  4. Open FitTrack from the new icon, then enable notifications in Settings if you want reminders.

Requires iOS 16.4 or newer for web push notifications.

What happens to your data

  • Everything you log is stored on your phone. There is no account and no signup.
  • Nothing is sent to a server, except the small amount needed to schedule a reminder notification.
  • It's free. There is no app store involved, so there is no payment screen to see.

A few things to know

  • Your log does not sync between devices. What you enter on your phone stays on your phone.
  • Clearing Safari website data will erase it. Use the export button in Settings inside the app once in a while to keep a backup.
