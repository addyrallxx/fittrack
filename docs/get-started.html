Get FitTrack on your phone

Free, no account, and everything stays on your phone. This page walks you through it once.

Open FitTrack https://addyrallxx.github.io/fittrack/fittrack.html

iPhone

Do these steps in order. On iPhone, notifications only work once FitTrack is added to your Home Screen. If you skip that and just keep using the link in a Safari tab, the notification toggle inside the app will still look like it turned on, but reminders will never arrive.

  1. Open the link above in Safari.

    Not Chrome, and not the browser built into Instagram, WhatsApp, or Messages. If this page opened inside one of those apps, look for a menu with three dots or a compass icon and choose "Open in Safari," or copy the link and paste it into Safari yourself.

  2. Tap the Share icon Share in Safari's toolbar.

    A square with an arrow pointing up out of it. It's usually at the bottom of the screen, or in the top bar on iPad.

  3. Scroll down the sheet that opens and tap "Add to Home Screen."

  4. Tap "Add" in the top corner.

    A FitTrack icon appears on your Home Screen, like any other app.

  5. Open FitTrack from that new icon, not from Safari.

    Only now go to Settings inside the app and turn on notifications. This is the step that actually works, because the app is no longer just a Safari tab.

Requires iOS 16.4 or newer. Most iPhones updated in the last couple of years already have it.

Android

  1. Open the link above in Chrome.

  2. Tap the three dots in the top right corner.

  3. Tap "Install app" (some phones show "Add to Home screen" instead).

Notifications work on Android either way, in a regular Chrome tab or installed. Installing still gives you a proper icon and a full screen app instead of a browser tab, so it's worth doing.

What happens to your data

  • Everything you log is stored on your phone. There is no account and no signup.
  • Nothing is sent to a server, except the small amount needed to schedule a reminder notification.
  • It's free. There is no app store involved, so there is no payment screen to see.

A few things to know

  • Your log does not sync between devices. What you enter on your phone stays on your phone.
  • Clearing Safari website data will erase it. Use the export button in Settings inside the app once in a while to keep a backup.
