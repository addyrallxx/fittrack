## What to make
`public/fittrack/music.wav`: exactly 30.000 s (1,323,000 samples), 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo, generated by a pure Node script with no samples, no downloads and no npm dependencies beyond Node itself. Deterministic: a seeded PRNG for any noise, so two runs produce byte-identical files (check it).

**Style:** a premium tech-commercial track at 120 BPM in A major, the register of an Apple product film. Clean, warm, confident, energetic. Not EDM, no harsh drops, no aggressive bass, no vocals. Chords one per bar from `chordsPerBar` (A, F#m, D, E), looping.

**Instruments to synthesize** (simple, well-shaped, mixed with care):
- Kick: sine with a fast pitch drop (about 150 Hz to 45 Hz over 60 ms), short click transient, exponential decay. Tight, not boomy.
- Clap: three staggered band-passed noise bursts (around 1.2 kHz) plus a short tail.
- Closed hat: high-passed noise, 30 ms decay, low in the mix, slight stereo spread.
- Sub bass: sine on the chord root, rhythmic eighths with gentle sidechain ducking from the kick.
- Pluck hook: a five-note phrase on the A major pentatonic that repeats every two bars, a bright plucked tone (decaying harmonics, or Karplus-Strong), panned slightly.
- Pad: detuned saw or triangle stack through a gentle low-pass, slow attack, filling the chords softly.
- SFX vocabulary for `cues`: `impact-soft` (low thump plus air), `swell` (filtered noise and pad rising over `dur`), `tick` (very short bright click), `whoosh` (band-passed noise sweep about 12 frames, stereo pan left to right), `whoosh-big` (longer, deeper), `hit` (kick plus clap plus a short bright chord stab), `hit-big` (the same, wider and louder, with a short reverb tail), `rise` (upward filtered sweep over `dur`), `tick-roll` (rapid ticks every 2 frames over `dur`, accelerating then settling), `tap` (soft UI tap), `confirm` (two-note rising chime, a fifth apart), `notify` (a gentle three-note bell), `key` (keyboard click; with `every` and `until`, repeat), `flip` (a quick reversed swish into a soft click), `land` (a soft thud with a slight squash feel), `riser` (noise plus pitch riser over `dur` ending exactly at the silence), `silence` (everything muted for `dur`, true digital silence), `boom` (deep sub boom with a long decay, plus shimmer), `bell-chord` (an A major bell chord that rings), `fade` (master fade to silence over `dur`).

**Arrangement:** follow `music` exactly: intro with pad and sub only; build with a kick every beat and the `hit` cues; groove with the full kit, sub, pluck hook and pad; breakdown where drums drop to a filtered kick and the riser climbs from 1500; 15 frames of true silence; finale where the hook returns big and wide and resolves to A major by frame 1680, ringing out, silent at 1800.

## Mastering and checks (write `scripts/synth-fittrack.check.mjs` for the numeric ones)
- Integrated loudness about -14 LUFS (plus or minus 1), true peak at or below -1 dBTP, no clipping. Measure with `ffmpeg -i public/fittrack/music.wav -af ebur128=peak=true -f null -` and paste the summary.
- Every cue's onset sits within 1 ms of its frame time: the check script recomputes each cue's sample index from timeline.json and asserts the script placed it there (export the placement table from the synth script or log it to JSON).
- The 15 frame silence measures as digital zero.
- Two consecutive runs give identical SHA-256.
- Log ends with `Final report`: the ffmpeg loudness summary, the SHA-256, and anything you simplified. No em dashes in anything you write.
