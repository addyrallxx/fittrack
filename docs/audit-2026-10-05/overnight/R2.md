## The music (Verso register, feedback-adnan.md rule 8)
100 BPM, warm major key (D major), 36.000 s, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo, pure Node, deterministic (seeded), no samples, no downloads.
- **Piano** carrying a simple, memorable motif of four or five notes: model it properly, not a sine beep: several partials with slight inharmonicity, a short hammer noise transient, per-partial exponential decays (higher partials faster), velocity-dependent brightness, a sustain tail, and a little stereo spread by pitch.
- **Warm analog pad:** detuned saw stack through a resonant low-pass with slow LFO movement, gentle chorus.
- **Pulse:** a soft, deep sub on each beat from the `pulse-and-percussion` section.
- **Airy percussion** from the same section: a shaker made from shaped noise, soft rim clicks, a light hat; never harsh.
- **Build:** a fuller pad and a rising melody line in the `build` section; the `payoff` bar lands a wide chord with a deep soft impact; `resolve` is a warm, uplifting final chord that rings out to silence.
- **Space:** a real reverb (implement Freeverb or a synthesized stereo impulse response with FFT convolution), more on piano and pad, less on percussion. Gentle bus compression and a limiter.
- **Foley** for every cue name in the timeline (`wake-click`, `shimmer`, `tap`, `tock`, `whoosh-up`, `snap`, `tick`, `piano-note` with `pitch`, `light-sweep`, `bloom`, `final-chord`): crisp, short, tasteful, mixed under the music, each landing exactly on its frame.
