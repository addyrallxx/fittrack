## Decisions
- **48 px:** every actionable control's hit area is at least 48 x 48 by real layout, never by overlapping invisible pseudo targets. The month calendar keeps seven columns: cells at least 48 px tall and as wide as the card allows; document that width exception in your log and in the feel gate.
- **Press policy, one for the whole app:** feedback starts on pointerdown through CSS `:active` (plus a `.pressed` class only where `:active` is unreliable), scale no lower than 0.98, tint or opacity for list rows, using the existing `--dur-press` and `--ease-press` tokens. Visible `:focus-visible` on every control; keyboard activation (Enter and Space) for every div or span that acts as a button, or make it a real `<button>`.
- **Tabular numerals** on every numeric label (rings, totals, timers, weights, chart pill, calendar, settings values).
- **Haptics:** one delegated helper near `buildTabs`. Android only (feature-detect `navigator.vibrate`, skip iOS and reduced motion): one 10 ms pulse per deliberate successful action (set check, add food, undo, save weight, sheet commit). Never for typing, scrolling, automatic refresh or navigation. The chart scrub keeps its own 8 ms ticks. On iOS, make the notification toggle a real `<input type="checkbox" switch role="switch">`: Safari 17.4 and later renders a native switch with the system haptic, so keep its native look there (feature-detect, do not set `appearance:none` on it in Safari) and keep the current custom look elsewhere.
- **Feel gate:** raise its target check from 44 to 48 px with the calendar width exception named explicitly. Raising the bar is allowed; never add a KNOWN entry.
