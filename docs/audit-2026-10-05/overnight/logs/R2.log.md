## Scope and decisions
- Read standing taste rules first, Verso reference, project README, v1 composition/timing and synthesis reference, and Remotion skill routes for 3D, fonts, sequencing, audio and rendering.
- Assigned output only: two R2 scripts, music-v2.wav, stems-v2/, and this log. No app changes, no server, no packages, no commit.
- Timeline source: src/projects/fittrack-v2/timeline.json, read only. 60 fps, 2160 frames, 100 BPM, 22 cues.
- Source timeline says A major and final A chord. The R2 brief expressly says D major. The score maps the source functional progression A/F#m/D/E to D/Bm/G/A and resolves to D. Cue pitches A5/C#6/E6 remain literal. Those notes are diatonic to D major.
- Built pure Node modal piano with 12 stretched partials, paired-string detuning, velocity brightness, filtered hammer excitation, soundboard mode, individual partial decays and sustained fundamental. Five-note motif D/F#/A/E/F#.
- Analog pad uses three band-limited detuned saws per pitch, resonant biquad low-pass with slow LFO, and interpolated stereo chorus.
- Pulse enters at frame 576. Airy shaker/rim/hat enters halfway (frame 1080, inside pulse-and-percussion). Build starts at 1152, wide D arrival/soft sub impact at 1728, final chord cue at 1872. Final fade follows the last 36 frames, ending with 10 ms of digital silence.
- Freeverb topology: 8 damped parallel combs and 4 serial allpasses per channel, stereo delay spread, 19 ms predelay. Piano/pad have more send than rhythm/foley. Linked gentle compressor, offline 5 ms lookahead limiter without a timing shift, seeded TPDF quantization.
- Stems are post-space and pre-master. Summing them gives the unmastered mix, not the mastered WAV. Manifest records cue samples, isolated hashes, arrangement events and source timeline hash.

## Final report

Delivered the complete deterministic D-major 100 BPM, 36-second score, sample-exact foley for every cue, four audit stems, a source-hashed manifest and a repeatable checker. All requested objective checks pass: -13.7 LUFS, -1.4 dBTP, 22 sample-exact cues, two byte-identical runs. No commits, new packages, app writes, paid tools, downloads or servers.

**Provisional Verso assessment: 6/10 overall, 9/10 for measurable structural compliance.** This is an engineering assessment, not a listening verdict. The motif, warmer synthesis, stereo room, restrained rhythm, rising build and two deliberate chord arrivals address v1's thin pluck/percussion-led design. The modal piano still lacks recorded piano detail: realistic pedal/string coupling, nuanced hammer contact, natural key releases and a real soundboard/room. Code synthesis here does not establish the premium Apple launch-film sound Adnan requested. Treat this as the finished zero-sample technical lane and a candidate score for morning audition, not certified final creative acceptance.
