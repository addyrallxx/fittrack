FitTrack now moves and responds like a native phone app.

- **Gym flow:** each set opens with what you lifted last time. One rest bar with minus and plus 15 s and Skip. The screen stays awake while you train.
- **Floor first:** with a calorie floor set, Home and Nutrition lead with the calories left to your floor.
- **Fast food logging:** your 12 recent foods in one tap, repeat a past meal, Undo on every add and delete.
- **Smooth everywhere:** tabs fade in place, screens keep their scroll, numbers update in place instead of replaying, sheets drag down to dismiss, and Android Back closes sheets first.
- **Chart:** drag across the weight chart to read any weigh-in.
- **Fixed:** your own carb and fat targets, streaks replaced by a calm weekly card, and Reduce Motion respected everywhere.

Updates install on their own the next time you open the app online. Full list in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/addyrallxx/fittrack/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
