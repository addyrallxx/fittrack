## The film
36 s, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps (2160 frames), 100 BPM (one beat 36 frames, one bar 144 frames, 15 bars), warm and premium. Apple product launch register: the device is the hero, light is the star, grounds alternate pure black `#000000` and off-white `#F5F5F7`, lots of negative space. Typography: `Inter Tight` 600 for centred main lines (white on black, `#1D1D1F` on off-white), sublines `Inter` 500 smaller in grey (`#A1A1A6` on black, `#86868B` on off-white), spec numbers huge and light (`Inter Tight` 200). Text fades in with a 12 px upward drift and out the same way. FitTrack accents (orange `#FF9500` to coral `#FF6B6B` ring) appear only in the app UI and the logo.

## Scenes (bars are 1-based; every scene a different camera move AND placement; you set exact frames in the timeline)
1. **Bars 1 to 2, black. "Rim light."** The phone stands vertical in darkness, rotating slowly (about 20 degrees); a thin rim light travels down its frame, revealing the silhouette. Main line "FitTrack." fades up below.
2. **Bar 3, black. "Wake and push."** The display wakes to dark-theme Home; the camera pushes in through the screen until the calorie ring fills the frame (a zoom-through into the UI at macro scale, shallow depth of field). Main "Your day." / sub "At a glance."
3. **Bars 4 to 5, off-white. "Flat-lay."** Match cut from the ring to a top-down view: the phone lies flat on off-white showing light-theme Workout; the camera rotates slowly above it and eases closer; a set checks (tap ripple, the row tints) and the rest timer appears. Main "One tap per set." / sub "Every rep counted."
4. **Bars 6 to 7, black. "Exploded UI" (set piece).** Phone at a three-quarter angle on dark-theme Nutrition. The screen's layers (header card, rings and bars, meal rows, search field) lift off the glass along Z into stacked floating planes with parallax and depth of field while the camera orbits about 25 degrees around them, then they glide back and snap into the screen. Main "Food, logged in two taps." / sub "Recent foods. Repeat a meal. Undo anything."
5. **Bar 8, off-white. "Numbers."** A slow lateral dolly past huge light spec numbers with the phone's frame out of focus in the foreground for parallax: "1,502" / "foods built in."; then "0" / "accounts."; then "$0" / "forever." One per beat pair.
6. **Bars 9 to 10, black. "The trend."** Phone vertical, slow orbit, Progress screen; a light sweep crosses the weight chart while the scrub hairline glides along the trend with its value pill; then push in on the line, which lifts off the glass as a glowing 3D ribbon of light floating in space (morph from 2D line to 3D ribbon). Main "The trend, not the noise."
7. **Bars 11 to 12, off-white. "Three phones."** Three phones stand side by side rotating in sync, staggered by 6 frames: dark-theme Home, light-theme Home, Nutrition. The camera pulls back wide. Small grey labels: "Dark", "Light", "Offline".
8. **Bar 13, black. The payoff (strongest moment).** The three phones turn away, revealing their camera plateaus; match cut from a lens ring to the FitTrack progress ring drawing itself large in orange light, with a soft bloom and one shockwave; main "Free. Offline. Yours."
9. **Bars 14 to 15, black. End card.** One phone lies horizontal and centred with a soft rim light; a final light sweep travels along its body from left to right; the FitTrack lockup (reuse v1's `FitTrackMark` and `Wordmark`) fades in, then "Install from your browser." in soft blue `#2997FF`, and tiny grey `addyrallxx.github.io/fittrack` at the bottom. Fade to black over the last 36 frames.
