## Decisions
- Find how the app knows a user is on a GLP-1 or has a floor (`settings.glp1`, `targets.floor`, the existing floor expression in `renderNutrition`). Do NOT change any floor maths or thresholds; change presentation only. Do not adopt invented rules such as time-of-day thresholds.
- Floor users, below the floor: the calorie hero leads with "<n> kcal to your floor" (large, tabular), protein to target second. At or above the floor: "Floor reached", with "<n> kcal left to your target" second. Never show a deficit as good news while intake is under the floor.
- Users without a floor: keep the target-led "remaining" presentation.
- Remove the duplicated metrics row under the Home rings (critique 1.2, audit F20) if it only repeats ring values.
- Changes animate only the delta (P4 contract), `tabular-nums`, no layout-property animation, no em dashes in copy.
